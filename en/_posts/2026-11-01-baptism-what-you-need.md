Organising a Baptism needs little, but it helps to start with the right things.

## First, the parish
Contact your parish: they will tell you the dates, the documents needed, the preparation meetings and what is provided during the celebration. Many choices depend on that.

## The elements of the celebration
- **White garment:** a sign of new life in Christ. It can be a christening gown, a small dress or a simple white blanket.
- **Baptismal candle:** in the rite it is lit from the Easter candle. You can choose a personalised candle with name and date, to keep as a memento.
- **Shell:** used in some celebrations to pour the water. Ask the priest whether the parish provides it.

## Keepsakes for guests
Favours and keepsakes are optional. A small angel, a memento scroll or a picture of a guardian angel are simple, appreciated choices.

## A tip
Choose the garment and candle first, then the keepsakes: that way the theme stays consistent and delivery times do not catch you out.
