The nativity scene we set up at home every year has a precise origin: Greccio, a small village in the province of Rieti, on Christmas night 1223.

## What happened at Greccio
Saint Francis of Assisi, recently back from the Holy Land, wanted to relive the birth of Jesus in Bethlehem in a concrete way. With the help of Giovanni Velita, the local lord, he had a manger prepared in a cave with hay, an ox and a donkey, and had Mass celebrated there at midnight. According to the sources, he first asked permission from Pope Honorius III.

## A nativity scene without figurines
That first nativity was a living scene, taken part in by local friars, farmers and shepherds. The figurines and crib scenes we know today came later, through a long artistic tradition that runs from Assisi and Rome to Naples.

## Where to see it today
At Greccio you can still visit the sanctuary with the Nativity Chapel, built into the cave where Francis set up the Nativity, and the event is remembered every year with a living nativity.

## The nativity at home
Setting up a nativity scene repeats that gesture: a few essential figures, the stable, the manger and some straw are enough. In our shop you will find figurines, stables and accessories in every size.
