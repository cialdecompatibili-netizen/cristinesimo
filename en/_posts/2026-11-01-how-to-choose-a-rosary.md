The traditional rosary has five decades of beads, each with an Our Father, ten Hail Marys and a Glory Be. Choosing the right one depends mostly on how you will use it.

## For daily prayer
Pick a rosary with beads that are easy to hold and a strong cord. Wood, such as olive wood, feels pleasant and does not slip. Beads that are too small or too smooth tire the hands during a long prayer.

## To carry with you
For a pocket or bag, choose a one-decade version, on a cord or bracelet, more compact and harder to damage.

## As a gift
For Baptism, Communion and Confirmation, rosaries in mother-of-pearl, crystal or glass are often chosen, with a case. For an adult, stone or wood beads with a crucifix and medal work well.

## Care and blessing
A priest can bless the rosary. Keep it dry and check now and then that the links and cord are intact.

## Where to find them
In the shop you will find olive wood and glass rosaries and pocket versions, as well as medals and chaplets.
