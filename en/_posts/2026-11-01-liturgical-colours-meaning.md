Anyone entering a church notices that the priest's vestments and the altar linens change colour during the year. It is not an aesthetic choice: each colour follows the liturgical calendar.

## White
The colour of joy and light. Used in the seasons of Christmas and Easter, on feasts of the Lord and of Mary, and for saints who are not martyrs.

## Red
It recalls blood and the fire of the Spirit. Used on Palm Sunday, Good Friday, at Pentecost and on feasts of apostles and martyrs.

## Green
The colour of Ordinary Time, the longest part of the liturgical year.

## Purple
It marks the seasons of waiting and penance: Advent and Lent. It may also be used at funerals.

## Rose
Optionally used on two Sundays of joy: the third Sunday of Advent and the fourth Sunday of Lent.

## Choosing vestments
For a parish it is best to start with white, red, green and purple, the colours used most often. Stoles, chalice veils and altar linens in these four colours cover almost the whole year. You will find these and other supplies in the Vestments and Linens section of the shop.
